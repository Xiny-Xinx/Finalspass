# FinalsPass

**AI-powered study assistant that turns your lecture materials into quizzes, Q&A, and study notes — in seconds.**

Upload your lecture slides (PPTX, PDF), and FinalsPass instantly:
- Generates practice quizzes to test your understanding
- Answers questions about the material with detailed explanations
- Creates concise study notes from dense academic content
- Remembers your learning history for spaced review

Built for university students who want to study smarter, not harder. No more staring at 50-slide decks wondering what to focus on.

- Upload once, study from any angle
- Works with your course materials, not generic content
- Typewriter-style AI responses for easy reading
- Full history of your learning sessions
